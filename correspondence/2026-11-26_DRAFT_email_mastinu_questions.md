Dear Pierfrancesco,

I have reworked the proposal. With two arms, the copper holder and about two days of beam, the run cannot compete with
MEG II, so I now present it as a test and calibration run. The aim is to show that the measured opening-angle
distributions agree with our Geant4 simulation, with the proton beam, the lithium target and its holder. If they agree,
we can propose the four-arm physics run for late 2027 with confidence. I will send you the new draft soon.

To finish the proposal I have some questions. The first ones matter most, because the beam-time request depends on them.

**The CN**

1. How many hours of beam per day does the CN give? The CN page says that it runs on working days, mainly in the
   daytime. I assume 8 hours of beam per working day, so 8 working days give 64 hours of beam. Is this right?
2. Is running in the evening, at night or at the weekend possible with an authorisation? If so, should we ask for it in
   the proposal?
3. Which CN line would we use? We need about 1.5 × 1.5 m² of free floor around the end of the line. What is the beam
   height, and does the existing copper hemisphere fit the end of that line?
4. Does LNL prefer June or July 2027 for us? If we are not ready by July, can the days move to the next period?

**To confirm (you said these should be possible)**

- An H₂⁺ beam at 0.883 MV, up to 0.5 µA, for the 441 keV resonance. Each H₂⁺ ion gives two protons of 0.441 MeV in the
  target. We expect some D⁺ in such a beam, since it has the same mass. Is there anything we should know about the D⁺
  level?
- Up to 4 µA of protons at 1.10 MeV on a spot of about 5 mm. Both energies are below the ⁷Li(p,n) threshold, so the
  target makes no neutrons. Should the proposal ask for up to 4 µA, or ask for 1 µA and mention that more is possible?

**The target and the γ monitor**

5. Can LNL measure the thickness and uniformity of each lithium film before the run (for example RBS, NRA or PIGE at a
   few spots, or by weighing)? How long does a film change on the CN line take, and how many spare films can we have?
6. Can we borrow a γ detector from the LNL pool to monitor the γ yield of the target? A LaBr₃ would be best, since it
   separates the 17.64 and 18.15 MeV lines and sees the 478 keV line. A NaI would also work.
7. Is there news on the thesis for the target holder? A lighter holder is the most important change for the physics
   run, so it would help to know when it could exist.

**The set-up**

8. Can the stand rotate the two arms by 90° about the beam? We want the arms vertical for the cosmic-ray nights and
   horizontal for the beam days.
9. For the liquid scintillator: how many litres do we have, which liquid is it, who remakes the cans and by when, and how
   many PMTs are there?
10. The Micromegas use a premixed Ar/iC₄H₁₀ (95/5) gas, which is flammable, at about 1 L/h. What are the LNL rules for
    its storage and exhaust in the hall?

**The proposal itself**

11. Who should be the spokesperson(s), and who is the GLIMOS? I understand the GLIMOS is normally local.
12. Could you send the affiliations of Massimi, Mengoni, Gervino, Gustavino and Cosentino? Should anyone else be on the
    author list?
13. The template says the body should not exceed 4 pages, but the LNL submission page says 5. I have kept to 4. Is that
    what people usually do?
14. You mentioned Commission II. Which INFN commission is it, and by when must that request be in?

Thank you very much. I am happy to discuss any of this by video call if it is easier.
