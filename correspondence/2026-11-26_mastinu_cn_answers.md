# 26 Nov 2026: Pierfrancesco's answers on the CN, the beam and the set-up

Reply in the CERN-mailbox thread "LNL PAC Proposal Call Dec 10" (26 Nov, 15:56), to the shorter version of the questions
that Dylan sent at 15:49 (the long draft in `2026-11-26_DRAFT_email_mastinu_questions.md` was not sent as is).

## Answers

1. **Beam hours.** The CN can run 24 hours a day, weekends included.
2. **CN line.** Not answered (we asked whether it is the 0° line, where his Li targets run).
3. **D⁺ in the H₂⁺ beam.** A small contamination could be present. He will ask the machine people for a number.
4. **Beam spot.** "5 mm diameter is even more than the FWHM": the spot FWHM is below 5 mm. Our simulations use a Gaussian
   with σ = 2 mm (FWHM 4.7 mm), so they are about right or slightly pessimistic. Up to 4 µA: not answered.
5. **γ monitor.** He has a BaF₂, 2 cm thick. Otherwise he can probably get a NaI(Tl), if it need not be too thick. He
   asks what γ energy we want to see. (Answer to send: 17.64 and 18.15 MeV, the ground-state γ yield and the
   17.64/18.15 share, and 478 keV for the Li content.)
6. **Liquid scintillator.** 70 L (we had assumed ~60 L).
7. **GLIMOS.** He has no idea who it is, and asks where we found it. (The abstract must give "the names of the Group Leader In Matter of Safety (GLIMOS)": this is on
   the LNL page "Details for submission" (https://www.lnl.infn.it/en/details-for-submissions/, re-read 27 Nov), not the .tex template.)

## Decisions taken from it (Dylan, 26 Nov)

- We count **beam days**: 24 h of CN running with **80 % operator efficiency** as a safety factor, so 19.2 h of beam per
  beam day. The proposal says this in one sentence (§III, "Use of the beam time").
- The request stays at 8 days, now 8 beam days (~150 h of beam). The day plan is unchanged: day 1 scan + 1.10 MeV,
  day 2 H₂⁺, days 3–7 at 1.10 MeV, day 8 arms at 90°. This gives 5.9 beam days (113 h) at 1.10 MeV with the arms opposite.
- All numbers were reworked (plan, page, proposal, figures): see `slot1_feasibility/CN_BEAM.md`, update of 26 Nov evening.
